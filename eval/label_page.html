Who is labelling?

Pick a short name (letters, digits, - _ .). Use the same name every time; each person labels every item on their own.

How to label

Each item is a group of Bluesky accounts that did something together in one time window. Decide from the evidence whether these accounts are acting together (coordinated) or independently (organic). You don't see what our detector said, and you shouldn't look it up on the dashboard.

  • Coordinated: hard to explain as independent people. Same text or link posted in a tight burst, fresh or empty accounts amplifying each other, clockwork timing, reply floods with one message, handles from one template.
  • Organic: plausibly independent people. Many accounts reacting to the same news, viral post, live event or fandom; normal conversation; old accounts with real followings doing ordinary things.
  • Can't tell: the evidence doesn't let you decide either way. That's a valid answer; don't guess.

We judge behaviour, not opinions: a group you disagree with can be organic, and a group you agree with can be coordinated. Open the bsky.app links if you need more context. Add a note when it was a close call.

Keys: 1/c coordinated, 2/o organic, 3/t can't tell (saves and moves on), n note, Esc leave note, →/j next unlabelled, ←/k previous, ? this help.
