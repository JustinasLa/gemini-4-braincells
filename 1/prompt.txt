### What the backend contract needs that you didn't specify
1. **Pings:** `: ping` comment lines can't be seen by `EventSource`. The client switches to polling after 25 s with no message. That means a Gemini reading longer than 25 s ends up on polling. That still works, it just costs more requests. Sending `event: ping` with `data: {}` instead keeps the stream open, and the client already listens for it.
2. **First snapshot:** it must arrive within 4 s of opening the stream, or the client falls back to polling. That is how it detects a proxy that buffers.
3. **Headers:** send `Cache-Control: no-cache` and `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, and make sure Caddy doesn't compress or buffer `text/event-stream` responses.
4. **Snapshot `id`:** it must equal the job id; on a mismatch the client closes the stream and polls. Leave out `retry:`, because the client closes the stream on any error and doesn't reconnect.
5. **Readings order:** `readings[0]` should be the same object as `assessment`. The client uses `readings` when it isn't empty, otherwise `assessment`. Counter-evidence comes only from `assessment`. If `readings_disagree` is missing, the client compares the `llm_reading` values itself.
6. **Second-reading snapshot:** don't change posts, pages, edges or clusters in the same snapshot as the Gemini reading. Any change to those reloads the graph and refits it.
7. **Timeout:** the 150 s limit covers Gemini too. If it runs out after Nemotron's reading, or the job fails or returns 404 then, the UI keeps the readings it has and shows a toast. It does not fall back to the old analysis.

- **Cache:**
  - The time-to-live cache expires entries on an injected clock and evicts the least recently used one.
  - A repeat call through `CachedOxylabs` does not reach the inner client and is marked cached. Failures are not cached, and each thread keeps its own `last_call`.

- **SSE:** a finished job sends one `snapshot` event equal to the snapshot, then the stream ends; an unknown id gives 404. A scripted running job sends only changed snapshots and stops at the first non-running one.
